# Walkocean

Trusted coastal walking companionship for older adults and families.

Walkocean is a wellness-first, elder-support, companionship, and caregiver-marketplace concept starting in **Bellingham / Whatcom County, Washington** with **Kelly Kenney** as the founding walker.

This repository now serves as the **main Phase 1 source** for:
- the deployable **Next.js website scaffold**
- the product planning docs
- the packaged Phase 1 project assets

## Phase 1 focus
Walkocean Phase 1 is a trust-first launch.

The goal is to help older adults and their families book safe, supportive coastal companionship walks while building the foundation for:
- website bookings
- family trust and reassurance
- PayPal USD payments
- future crypto payments
- subscriptions and booking fees
- later expansion into a broader local companion marketplace

- **Positioning:** wellness, elder support, companionship, caregiver marketplace
- **Launch geography:** Whatcom County
- **Founder / first walker:** Kelly Kenney
- **Primary audience:** older adults and their families
- **Core promise:** trusted, scheduled coastal walking companionship

## Claims guardrail
Walkocean should be marketed around:
- movement
- companionship
- time outdoors
- routine
- confidence
- wellbeing

Avoid unsupported medical claims about ocean air curing illness, directly boosting immunity, or guaranteeing longevity.
