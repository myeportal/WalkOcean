# Walkocean Product Brief

## Confirmed inputs
- Project name: Walkocean
- Domain: Walkocean.com
- Founder / first walker: Kelly Kenney
- Launch region: Bellingham, Washington / Whatcom County
- Product surfaces: website + mobile app
- App targets: Apple App Store and Google Play
- Payments: USD via PayPal, crypto option, plus platform rake on bookings
- Positioning mix: wellness, elder support, companionship, caregiver marketplace
- Delivery mode: build website and app planning in parallel

## Core concept
Walkocean helps older adults and their families book trusted companionship walks in coastal areas, starting with Whatcom County. The service combines gentle outdoor walking, local companionship, and a safer support layer for people who want movement, routine, and confidence near the ocean.

## Recommended positioning
Primary: trusted coastal walking companionship for older adults
Supporting: wellness, elder support, family reassurance, local caregiver marketplace

## Safer claim strategy
Use outcomes around movement, consistency, companionship, time outdoors, routine, confidence, and wellbeing.
Avoid hard medical claims about sea air curing illness or making specific immune/circulatory changes unless supported by high-quality clinical evidence for the exact claim.

## Problem statement
Many older adults want safe, gentle outdoor movement and companionship but do not want to walk alone. Their families also want more confidence that loved ones can get exercise, fresh air, and human connection with a trusted person.

## MVP promise
Book a trusted local walking companion for a safe, scheduled coastal walk.

## Primary user groups
1. Older adults who want companionship, confidence, and a reason to get outside
2. Adult children / family members arranging support for a parent or loved one
3. Qualified local walkers / companions, starting with Kelly Kenney

## MVP scope
- Region selector constrained to Whatcom County at launch
- Founder profile for Kelly Kenney
- Booking flow for 30/60 minute walks
- Preferences for pace, mobility, and route comfort
- Emergency contact capture
- Check-in / check-out status
- PayPal checkout for USD
- Crypto checkout option
- Subscription or package options for repeat walks
- Simple admin dashboard for bookings and status

## Revenue model
- One-off booking fee
- Subscription or recurring package for frequent walkers
- Small platform percentage rake on each booking
- Optional premium add-ons later: family notifications, recurring schedules, concierge matching

## Marketplace rollout plan
Phase 1: single-founder service model with Kelly only
Phase 2: small trusted local companion network
Phase 3: marketplace expansion to additional coastal regions

## Website goals
- Build trust with families
- Explain the service clearly
- Convert visitors into bookings or consult calls
- Capture waitlist demand for expansion areas
- Create a strong app-store-ready brand foundation

## App goals
- Simple account creation
- Book and manage walks
- Pay easily
- Receive reminders and confirmations
- Provide caregiver/walker side workflow in later phase

## Suggested stack direction
- Website/admin: Next.js on Vercel
- Mobile app: React Native with Expo
- Backend/data/auth: Supabase or Firebase
- Payments first: PayPal
- Crypto second: stablecoin or wallet-based checkout after clean policy review

## Immediate build order
1. Trust-first website with booking funnel
2. Booking/admin backend
3. Mobile app MVP for customer side
4. Walker-side tools
5. Multi-walker marketplace layer
