# Walkocean Site and App Strategy

## Brand direction
Calm, premium, trustworthy, coastal, local, elder-safe, family-approved.

## Core message
Trusted companionship walks by the ocean for older adults who want movement, confidence, and human connection.

## Website sitemap
1. Home
2. How It Works
3. Safety & Trust
4. Meet Kelly
5. For Families
6. Pricing
7. Coverage Area / Routes
8. FAQ
9. Book a Walk / Join Waitlist

## Homepage section order
- Hero
- Social proof / local trust
- How it works
- Why movement + companionship matter
- Meet Kelly Kenney
- Safety & trust
- Pricing / plans
- Coverage area
- FAQ
- Final CTA

## Core app flows
### Customer / family side
- Browse service
- Choose date/time
- Set walker preferences
- Add mobility and emergency contact info
- Pay
- Get reminders
- Confirm completion

### Walker side
- Accept schedule
- View client notes
- Start check-in
- Complete walk
- Log completion note

## MVP trust features
- Background-check badge if true and documented
- Emergency contact
- Check-in / check-out timestamps
- Route preference notes
- Weather-aware scheduling
- Cancellation / refund clarity

## Monetization model
- Single walk booking
- Weekly or monthly subscription
- Small platform fee on each booking
- Expansion option for caregiver marketplace rake later

## Local launch strategy
- Make Whatcom County feel deeply local
- Use Kelly as the face of the brand
- Capture expansion interest from nearby coastal areas
- Emphasize trust, consistency, and ease for families

## First build recommendation
- Launch trust-first site before overbuilding the marketplace
- Use the site to validate demand, booking frequency, pricing, and retention
- Then convert proven flows into the mobile app experience
