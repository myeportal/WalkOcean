# Walkocean Founder Handoff

## What Walkocean is
Walkocean is a local wellness and elder-support service that helps older adults and families book trusted companionship walks by the ocean, starting in Bellingham and Whatcom County.

## Founder model
- Founder / first walker: Kelly Kenney
- Initial supply model: Kelly-first launch
- Expansion path: trusted local walker network, then broader marketplace

## Phase 1 positioning
- wellness
- elder support
- companionship
- caregiver marketplace

## Core promise
Book a trusted walking companion for a safe, scheduled coastal walk.

## Payment model
- USD via PayPal
- crypto option
- subscription plans
- small platform fee / rake on bookings

## Guardrails
Use wellness and companionship language.
Do not use unsupported medical claims about ocean air, immunity, or longevity.
