# Walkocean Evidence Notes

Use these notes to keep marketing claims safe and grounded.

## Stronger, safer themes
- Regular physical activity supports healthy aging.
- Walking is an accessible form of activity for many older adults.
- Physical activity can support function, independence, and wellbeing in older adults.
- Social connection matters for older adults.
- Nature exposure and blue/green spaces are associated with mental wellbeing, but avoid overstating causal medical claims.

## Avoid without stronger proof
- Ocean air boosts white blood cell count.
- Sea air directly improves circulation in a clinically proven universal way.
- Walkocean prevents disease.
- Walkocean helps people live to 100.

## Marketing translation
Say:
- helps older adults stay active with support
- encourages time outdoors and movement
- offers companionship and confidence
- provides a gentle routine many families value

Avoid:
- cure, treat, prevent, heal
- clinically proven sea air claims unless sourced for that exact point
